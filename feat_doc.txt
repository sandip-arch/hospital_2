Hospital Management System (HMS) — Features & RBAC Requirements
1. Core Hospital Management System Modules & Features
To efficiently operate a hospital, a system must handle clinical, administrative, operational, and financial workflows. Below are the 10 core modules and their essential features:
1. User & Access Control Management
User Management: Registration, authentication, multi-factor authentication (MFA), password resets.
Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Defining roles, assigning permissions, managing access levels.
Audit Trail & Logging: Tracking user logins, critical data edits, and compliance logging.
2. Patient Management (ADT — Admission, Discharge, Transfer)
Patient Registration: Capturing personal info, emergency contacts, insurance details, and documents.
Medical Identity: Unique Patient Identification (UPI) generation.
Outpatient & Inpatient Tracking: Managing OPD visits, hospital admissions, transfers, and discharge summaries.
3. Doctor & Clinical Management (EMR / EHR)
Digital Consultation Notes: SOAP (Subjective, Objective, Assessment, Plan) notes, diagnosis coding.
Electronic Prescriptions (e-Prescribing): Standardized drug dosage, duration, and instructions.
Order Entry: Ordering lab tests, radiology scans, and procedures.
4. Appointment & Scheduling Management
Doctor Roster & Availability: Managing shifts, time slots, and leave calendars.
Booking Engine: Online/counter appointment booking, rescheduling, and cancellations.
Queue Management: Real-time patient queue tracking in waiting areas.
5. Hospital Facilities & Bed Management
Ward & Room Directory: Categorization of ICU, Private, Semi-Private, and General wards.
Bed Tracker: Real-time visibility of available, occupied, cleaning, or maintenance beds.
Admissions & Transfers: Seamless bed assignment for admitted patients.
6. Laboratory & Diagnostics Management
Test Catalog & Pricing: List of available lab tests and radiology scans.
Sample Tracking & Processing: Status updates (requested -> in_progress -> completed).
Report Generation & Upload: Attaching lab test reports and digital radiology files.
7. Pharmacy & Inventory Management
Drug Master Catalog: Medicine listings with unit pricing, categories, and generic names.
Stock & Inventory Control: Low-stock alerts, expiration tracking, stock batching.
Prescription Fulfilling: Direct integration with doctor prescriptions for dispensing.
8. Billing & Financial Management
Consolidated Invoicing: Automated itemized charges (consultation, room daily rates, lab, medicine).
Payment Processing: Support for cash, credit/debit cards, UPI, and bank transfers.
Insurance & Claims: Tracking claim status and partial patient payments.
9. Communication & Notifications
Alert System: Automated SMS/Email/In-App reminders for appointments, bills, and lab results.
Internal Messaging: Secure communication channels between doctors and staff.
10. System Administration & Analytics
Master Configurations: Setting department lists, tax rates, operational hours.
Executive Dashboards: Revenue reports, bed occupancy rates, patient throughput metrics.
2. Overview of System Roles
superadmin: System administrator responsible for system configuration, user provision, security policies, and high-level reporting.
doctors: Healthcare providers focused on diagnosing patients, prescribing treatments, reviewing lab tests, and managing clinical notes.
staff: Operational and clinical support teams (Nurses, Receptionists, Pharmacists, Lab Technicians, Cashiers).
patients: Individuals receiving care who access their personal health records, appointments, and billing via a patient portal.
users: Unverified or prospective users (e.g., guest visitors registered on the portal before being verified or linked to a patient profile).

4. Role Responsibility Breakdown
1. Superadmin
Primary Objective: Ensure platform operational security, compliance, and system integrity.
Managed Features:
Manage system users, roles, and granular permissions (role_user, permission_role).
Configure hospital departments, fee structures, tax settings, and system defaults.
Access security audit logs, user login activities, and hospital-wide analytical reports.
Oversee master data setup (medicines master list, lab test catalogs).
2. Doctors
Primary Objective: Provide clinical care, diagnose conditions, and prescribe medical regimens.
Managed Features:
Set and adjust personal consultation time slots and weekly availabilities.
Review assigned daily appointment schedules and queue status.
View complete clinical history of assigned patients.
Create diagnosis entries, record vital sign observations, write clinical notes, and order lab tests.
Issue e-prescriptions detailing dosages, duration, and instructions.
Authorize patient admission and discharge orders.
3. Staff (Sub-role Functionalities)
Primary Objective: Facilitate day-to-day administrative, clinical support, and financial operations.
Managed Features by Staff Sub-Roles:
Receptionist / Front Desk: Register new patients, handle walk-in bookings, manage appointment check-ins.
Nurses: Record patient vitals, monitor bed assignments, manage ward duty lists.
Lab Technicians: Receive lab test requests, input test parameters, attach diagnostic file reports.
Pharmacists: Review prescriptions, verify drug stock, update inventory quantities, dispense drugs.
Billing Desk / Cashier: Generate combined patient invoices, collect payments across payment channels, issue receipts.
4. Patients
Primary Objective: Engage with personal health details, book appointments, and settle medical bills.
Managed Features:
View personal profile, medical documents, and emergency contacts.
Search doctor directories by department/specialization and schedule appointments.
View historical medical records, diagnosis summaries, and doctor prescriptions.
Access lab test results as soon as they are published.
View invoice breakdowns and pay outstanding fees online.
5. Users (Guest / Unverified Accounts)
Primary Objective: Basic portal navigation prior to patient profile verification.
Managed Features:
View public hospital information, available departments, and listed doctors.
Self-register for an account on the public portal.
Submit a request to book an initial appointment or request profile linking to an existing offline patient record.
